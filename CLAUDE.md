# Sandbox Tracker — Session Rules

Read DESIGN.md at the start of every session. It is the working spec.

**Keep CASE_STUDY.md current.** Whenever a design decision is made or changed in conversation, add a dated entry to CASE_STUDY.md before ending the turn. Capture mostly design decisions: what was decided, the options considered, why one path was chosen over the others, and anything that changed the plan. Write it so a portfolio reader can follow the reasoning. Keep DESIGN.md in sync (current state only, no history).

Do not inflate claims: describe the project at the stage it is really at.

Monetization is part of design from the start, not a late step.
